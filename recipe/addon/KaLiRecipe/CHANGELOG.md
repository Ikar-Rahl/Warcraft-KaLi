# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (not released yet)

First version.

- Recipes noted at vendors, trainers, the auction house, on loot, as quest rewards and on arriving in the bags; one chat line per visit.
- A sound when a visit notes a recipe new to the notebook, the quest log's quill, and a shorter one for a new place of a recipe already in it, the same quill stopped early: the game's own sounds, one per visit, each chosen in the options (with two parchments) or turned off.
- The vendor's and the trainer's price for each recipe, kept with where it was seen and shown in the detail and the recipe's tooltip; the trainer's has its own option.
- What each character knows (the professions window, the trainer, the spellbook, a recipe's « Déjà connu ») and holds (bags and bank), shared by all your characters.
- « Carnet : N à apprendre · N vues » in the professions window and the professions book.
- The notebook: three sections by the game's categories, search and filters, the recipe's detail below the list, two styles with each profession's art, a corner grip to resize it, « Suivre » docking it on the professions window, profession tabs.
- Marks at vendors, in bags (and Baganator's), at the auction house; crafted items' profession icon. At the auction house, the marks sit at the end of the quantity's column, and the addon searches only when « Relever les recettes » is clicked, never by itself. The option « À l'hôtel des ventes » turns the button and the marks off there; recipes listed are still noted.
- Tooltip lines for recipes, reagents and crafted items.
- World map pins and waypoints.
- Options in the game's settings with a reset for each section; the addon compartment; a minimap button, which can be hidden or locked; a launcher for information bars such as Titan Panel; `/kr`.
- A bug report to copy, with a check at login that the game still has all the addon uses and answers as it assumes.
- Saved data that survives: each character's file keeps a copy of the notebook, which brings it back if the account's file goes missing; nothing a later version adds is lost, and nothing unreadable is deleted, only set aside; options keep only what you changed. `/kr data` shows its state.
