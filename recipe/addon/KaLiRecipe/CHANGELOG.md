# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (not released yet)

First version.

- Recipes noted at vendors, trainers, the auction house, on loot, as quest rewards and on arriving in the bags; one chat line per visit.
- A sound when a visit notes a recipe new to the notebook, the quest log's quill, and a shorter one for a new place of a recipe already in it, the same quill stopped early: the game's own sounds, one per visit, each chosen in the options (with two parchments) or turned off. « Volume des sons » sets both, from 0 to 300% of the game's own level for them, 200% by default; the game's own volumes still apply.
- The vendor's and the trainer's price for each recipe, kept with where it was seen and shown in the detail and the recipe's tooltip; the trainer's has its own option.
- A grey clock after a vendor's name when its stock of the recipe was limited, wherever the name shows.
- What each character knows (the professions window, the trainer, the spellbook, a recipe's « Déjà connu ») and holds (bags and bank), shared by all your characters.
- « Carnet : N à apprendre · N vues » in the professions window and the professions book.
- The notebook: three sections by the game's categories, search and filters, the recipe's detail below the list, two styles with each profession's art, a corner grip to resize it, « Suivre » docking it on the professions window, profession tabs.
- Marks at vendors and in bags (and Baganator's); crafted items' profession icon. None on the auction house's rows, Blizzard's or Auctionator's: there the recipe's tooltip says it all, and the addon searches only when « Relever les recettes » is clicked, never by itself.
- Tooltip lines for recipes, reagents and crafted items.
- World map pins and waypoints.
- Options in a window of their own, in the notebook's « Épuré » look, beside the notebook, which stays open as they're set: folding sections, « Annuler les modifications » and « Par défaut ». The notebook's gear opens it; the game's settings have a button to it. The addon compartment; a minimap button, which can be hidden or locked; a launcher for information bars such as Titan Panel; `/kr`.
- Its own icon, in KaLi Quest's black and gold: a K and a split feather.
- A bug report, from the notebook's gear, the options or `/kr report`: a link that opens a new issue, then the report to copy and paste into it; with a check at login that the game still has all the addon uses and answers as it assumes.
- In French, English, German and Spanish (Spain's, and Mexico's with the game's own Mexican words); other clients get English.
- Saved data that survives: each character's file keeps a copy of the notebook, which brings it back if the account's file goes missing; nothing a later version adds is lost, and nothing unreadable is deleted, only set aside; options keep only what you changed. `/kr data` shows its state.
