KaLi HUD's icon

The family's frame, KaLi Quest's « Noir et or » (black tile, thin gold corners, the gold K in Friz), with a sign of the HUD where KaLi Quest has its quest mark and KaLi Recipe its feather; then four where the K stands in the HUD itself. Drawn 64 by 64; the game shrinks the file to 20 px in the AddOns list and 16 px in the compartment. Click a candidate to see it there beside its two siblings. The sign can be gold, as theirs, or the plate's own bronze. Make one the addon's with python -I hud/tools/make_icon.py <id> (not written yet: make_media.py still writes Media/Icon.tga and must stop first).
