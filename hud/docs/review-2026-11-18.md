# KaLi HUD: review of 2026-11-18

Ikar: "Do a full review of the kali hud addon. In depth. Stability, solidity, maintainability."

This one is about the code and how it is kept, not about features. It follows the reviews of 2026-11-16 and
2026-11-17 (`review-2026-11-17.md`, `design.md`'s last two sections) and repeats nothing they settled: the plate
static outside Edit Mode, the twin buttons, the proposals already ruled.

Nothing was changed in the addon while reviewing. Each finding has a number so it can be ruled by number.

**Ruled the same day** (Ikar): "Refactor, as much as you think is needed. And fix all except first two." V1 (version
control) and V2 (a run in the game) stay his. R1 to R7, V3, M1, M2 and M4 are done, M3 in part; `design.md`'s section
"The review of 2026-11-18" says how, and what was left as it is. Checked offline only.

## In short

- **The code defends itself well where it was taught to.** Every game event, every timer and everything that runs
  each frame is guarded; nothing is laid out in a fight; the saved data is repaired at load and has its own tests.
- **Seven new weaknesses, six of them shown by a probe on the fake game.** None is met in ordinary play today. They
  are about how much is lost the day something unexpected happens: one of them loses the whole bar (R1), one loses
  every piece of the HUD without a word (R3).
- **The two largest risks are not in the code.** The whole `hud/` folder and its 507 deliberate breaks are still not
  under version control (V1), and nothing has run in the game yet (V2).
- **Maintainability: the code itself is readable and consistent; the cost is in the tests and in how tightly the
  info bar's files hold each other.** One smoke session of 6,270 lines carries 867 checks, and each of the 507 breaks
  replays all of it (M1). The saved places are written from 18 places in 6 files (M2).

- **Read in part or not at all:** `Infos/Stats.lua` (its outline only) and `Skills.lua`, `IconPicker.lua`, the second
  half of `MacroEditor.lua`, the texts of `Locale.lua`, the data tables, `KaLiHUD_Probe`, the fake game itself, the

- **Reproduced** means a probe started the addon on the repo's fake game, as `hud/tools/run_tests.py` does, and

- **Counted** figures come from a script over the source (appendix). They are rough where a pattern stands for a

### Solidity

**V1. Not under version control.** `git status` on 2026-11-18: `?? hud/` and `?? tools/breaks/hud.py`, on branch
`update-2026-11-13`. That is 30,000 lines of Lua, the tests, the 507 breaks, both reviews and `design.md`, the
decision log. A mistaken command, a disk fault or a bad edit of mine has no way back. Seventeen files of the other
addons and of the shared tools are modified and uncommitted beside it. Committing is Ikar's to ask for; I recommend
it before any further change.

**V2. Nothing has run in the game.** Two reviews, 867 offline checks and 507 breaks rest on a fake game that has
hidden real defects before (`design.md`, the review of 2026-11-16). Since the last review six features were added
on inference: the flash, the reputation folder (unticking the watched faction passes 0, taken from retail), the
clock's two CVars, the new measure, the looks, the landing of new addons' readouts. `review-2026-11-17.md`'s section 2
still gives the fifteen-minute test; `/kh test` then `/khprobe` have no run on record.

**V3. A repair nobody is told of.** `DB.Open` counts what it put right or dropped (`info.fixed`), and
`Core/Main.lua:22` writes it to the debug log, which is kept only while debug mode is on. `/kh status` and the bug
report say "version 1" and nothing more (`Core/Commands.lua:16`). A player whose file lost folders or places to a
repair has no trace of it, and neither has the report he sends. Fix: keep the last repair's count and date in
`KaLiHUDDevDB`, show them in the status and the report.

### Stability

**R1. Another addon's button that can't be placed takes the bar with it.**
- The last review guarded each element's *drawing* (`HubButtons.SafeFill`, D5). *Placing* is not guarded where the
  frame is someone else's: `InfoBar.lua`'s `put` calls `ns.Collect.Place` and `ns.Natives.PlaceOnBar` bare (lines
  561 to 565), `placeRound` too (696), and `collect` calls `Collect.GiveBack` and `Collect.Hide` (348 to 362).
  `Collect.Place` (`Collect.lua:348`) makes at least seven calls on the other addon's frame.
- Reproduced by making the fake's plain map button refuse `SetPoint`, standing on the bar's left: the bar went from
  22 items placed to 1, and stopped following (gold sent to the right end stayed drawn where it was).
- A folder's list is safe: its layout is guarded as a whole. The bar and the map's ring are not.
- No real button was found that does this today. As with D5, it is about how much is lost the day one does.
- A side effect: `Collect.Place` sets `moving = f` before its calls and clears it after (356 to 367). An error
  between the two leaves it set, and that frame's own move is never followed again this session.
- Fix: guard each such placement as `SafeFill` guards a drawing; one that fails is given back to its addon and left
  out of this layout, the error kept once; `moving` cleared whatever happens.

**R2. An action that fails half way.**
- `History.Do` (`History.lua:168`) takes a copy of the saved tables, runs the action under `pcall`, and on failure
  raises the error again with `error(a, 0)` (line 177).
- Reproduced with an action that moves gold and then fails: gold's saved side stayed changed ("left" to "free"), no
  undo step was recorded, and the error kept in `/kh errors` named `History.lua` and not the function that failed.
- So two things are lost: the way back, though the copy to put the data back from is in hand at that moment, and
  the stack, which is what the bug report is for. Every drag and every menu action goes through here.
- Fix: run the action under `xpcall` with the stack taken where it failed; on failure put back what the copy held
  (the same `put` an undo uses), redraw, then report.

**R3. A piece that didn't load whole.**
- `Core/Main.lua:57` starts the pieces from a list built in place:
  `for _, start in ipairs({ ns.Bags.Start, ns.Windows.Start, ... })`. A file that fails while loading, after its
  `ns.X = X` line, leaves `ns.X.Start` nil. A nil in the list ends `ipairs` there.
- Reproduced by taking `ns.Windows.Start` away before login: no status bars, no plate, no info bar, no options page,
  and **zero errors kept**. The game shows a load error only when its own error display is on, which it is not by
  default.
- The comment above says "Each piece starts on its own, so one that fails leaves the others". That holds for an
  error while starting, not for a piece that isn't there.
- Fix: a list of names; each looked up and started under guard; one missing kept as an error that names it. The login
  self-check can list the pieces it expects.

**R4. Errors the addon never hears of.**
- Game events, timers and every-frame work all pass through `ns.SafeCall` or `ns.EveryFrame`. Script handlers and
  menu callbacks mostly do not. Counted: 85 handlers written in place on a `SetScript`, `HookScript`,
  `hooksecurefunc` or `RegisterCallback`; about half neither guard their body nor merely ask for a later redraw.
  Handlers passed by name are not in that count and are mostly bare too.
- The ones that run real logic: the fold button's click (`Windows.lua:289` to `Windows.Toggle`, a plate redraw), the
  padlock's click and the bar's right-click (`InfoBar.lua:230`, `1071`), our buttons' own `onEnter`, `onClick` and
  drag handlers (`Buttons.lua:116` to `226`, which guard the element's callbacks but not their own body), the status
  bars' menu (`StatusBars.lua:683`), and the hub's menus: `Menus.AddEntries` guards its entries, `Menus.Element` and
  `Menus.Bar` call `History.Do` bare (`Menus.lua:155` to `216`, `356` to `411`).
- The game catches such an error, so nothing else breaks. But it goes to the game's handler alone: `/kh errors` and
  the bug report don't have it, and the chat line that tells the player never shows. With the game's error display
  off and no error addon, it is silent.
- Fix: one wrapper for a handler (as `ns.EveryFrame` is for `OnUpdate`), used everywhere; and an offline check that
  fails a bare handler in the shipped code, as one already fails a value compared with itself.

**R5. What is made once.**
- `Seeds.Run` (`Seeds.lua:412`) runs seven steps in a row with no guard between them, and each sets its once-flag
  before doing its work (`seedEmotes`: line 229, then the folder and its macros).
- Reproduced on a first run by making the first folder's placing fail: the « Emote » folder was made empty and none
  of the ten others that session; at the next login the ten were made, and « Emote » stayed empty for good, its flag
  set. Only its menu's reset fills it.
- `Hub.Start` already runs its three steps apart since D1; the same rule stops one level short.
- Fix: each step under its own guard; a flag set once its work is done.

**R6. A folder's key.**
- `DB.Open` checks that a folder's key is a text (`DB.lua:231`), not that it is a folder's id. `Folders.IsFolder` is
  "saved under that key"; the button's kind comes from `Folders.IsId`, the pattern `KaLiHUD_Folder<n>`
  (`Folders.lua:66`, `Buttons.lua:229`). A folder under another key gets a plain button, `Folders.Wire` never runs,
  and `Folders.Press` reads a well that isn't there (`Folders.lua:930`).
- Reproduced with `folders = { Outils = { name = "Outils" } }`: loaded with nothing repaired, then the same error at
  every layout (3 after login, 5 after two more). A macro so saved is a button that runs nothing.
- It needs a file edited by hand or damaged, as D12 did. Fix: `DB.Open` drops a folder, a macro or a separator whose
  key isn't its kind's id, counted as repaired; a test in `Test_DB.lua`.

**R7. The info bar off, and a twin.**
- A map button whose addon also has a broker object of the same name is put away, the object standing for it
  (`Collect.lua:258` to `281`; ruled 2026-11-14 for KaLi Recipe's). With the info bar off, LibDBIcon's buttons and
  plain ones go back on the map (the review of 2026-11-16). A twin does not.
- Reproduced: bar off, KaLi Recipe's button stays in the hidden holder while LibDBIcon's and the plain one are back
  on the map; its object's button shows nowhere. KaLi Recipe is out of reach until the bar is on again.
- Fix: a twin kept in a list and given back while its object shows nowhere, as `SyncIcons` does for LibDBIcon's.

**Smaller notes, read only.**
- `Windows.Start` hooks `ChatFrame1EditBox` alone. With the chat style « messagerie instantanée » (`chatStyle`
  "im", in this build's settings) each docked window types in its own box: with another tab picked, a folded chat
  would not unfold to show what is typed. To see in game.
- An element whose drawing failed shows the question mark, and `Buttons.Failed` exists "for the report", but
  `Hub.Text` doesn't call it: the report doesn't say which element.
- `StatusBars`' `hearAgain` gives the mouse back to frames with no check for a fight, where `deafen` has one
  (`StatusBars.lua:152`). Only if one of Blizzard's bar's children were protected.
- `Shared/Page.lua`'s `Build` marks the page built before building it: a row of an unknown kind leaves half a page
  for the session.
- `Border.Update` reads `LOOK.thin.inset` where `Border.Profile` allows for no `LOOK.thin`.
- Six tickers run whether their item shows anywhere or not (the speed four times a second, hidden by default).
  A few calls a second: noted, not worth a change.

### Maintainability

**M1. The tests' shape.**
- What is there is strong: static checks of every game call, method, enum, atlas and sound against this build;
  58 unit tests on the parts with no game call (the saved data, the numbers, the plate's shape); the smoke; a restart
  from the file the smoke wrote; a first run with no other addon; the labels measured in four languages.
- The cost: `hud/tools/lua/smoke.lua` is one session of 6,270 lines and 867 checks. Each block starts from whatever
  the blocks before it left. On 2026-11-17 the rule for where new readouts land broke several older checks that had
  nothing to do with it, and Lua's limit of 200 locals in one chunk has been hit.
- The 507 breaks patch the source by its exact text and each replays the whole session. A full pass takes hours,
  which is why it is no longer run (Ikar, 2026-11-17: "You have spent hours running checks"). A rename or a
  reformatting makes breaks stale; the stale check says so, and each must be rewritten by hand.
- Direction, in steps, when the info bar is next worked on: scenarios that each start from a login (the runner
  already builds a fresh game for the restart and for the first run), so a new rule can only break its own; each
  break naming the scenario that must catch it, so it replays seconds and not the whole; and the logic that needs no
  frame (`Hub.Insert` and `Step`, the pockets' places, `History`'s diff, what doesn't fit) moved to unit tests.

**M2. Who writes the saved places.**
- `ns.db.items[name]` is each element's place. `Hub.lua`'s `write` knows its rules (a pocket's slot only in a
  pocket, a folder only for one in a folder or hidden from one, the angle and the position kept in range). Counted:
  18 direct writes in 6 files go round it (`Seeds.lua` 7, `Folders.lua` 5, `Hub.lua` 3, `Collect.lua`, `Macros.lua`,
  `Separators.lua` 1 each), plus fields set on a saved place in `Folders.Delete` and `Seeds`' refill.
- Nothing was found wrong in them. The cost comes with each new field: `look`, added on 2026-11-17, had to be thought
  through against each of those places (a folder's reset drops it, a folder's deletion keeps it), and only the load's
  repair checks the result, at the next login.
- Direction: `Hub` owns the table, with a few named operations (place, hide, forget, pin as it stands); the others
  call them.

**M3. Size and coupling.** 52 files of logic, 20,435 lines, 1,171 functions. Fifteen functions run 80 lines or
more: `Folders.Layout` 194, the macro editor's `build` 189, `DB.Open` 167, `Stats`' `sourcesOf` 138, the status
bars' `build` 127, `Plate.Layout` 97, `Menus.Element` 91. Through `ns`, `Folders.lua` reaches 14 other modules,
`InfoBar.lua`, `Hub.lua` and `Drag.lua` 11 each, in rings (Hub, InfoBar, Folders, Seeds, Macros reach one another).
A layout's result travels as fields put on the buttons themselves (`_pass`, `ux`, `uy`, `_mode`, `_width`,
`slotSide`, `secure`, `pressed`, `kaliWell`, `kaliDisarmed`, `_dragged`, `textColor`), written by four files. It
works and is commented; it means a change to the bar's layout is a change to read in five files.

**M4. Three copies.** `Folders.lua`, `Macros.lua` and `Separators.lua` each carry the same prefix, `number`,
`byNumber`, `List`, `Forget` and "first free id" code; two carry the same `clean` and the same table of glyphs.

**Smaller notes.**
- The saved data's version has stayed 1; its migrations run inline at every load, and a dozen retired option names
  (`DB.lua:372`) can never be used again for something new.
- A ruling is written in the code's comments, in `design.md`, and often in the README, the changelog and a review.
  The clock's change of 2026-11-17 touched texts in seven files. It is the project's way and it explains the code
  well; it is also where two places can come to disagree.

## 2. What holds, by reading

- **Guards.** Every event goes through `ns.Fire`, each handler under its own guard; every timer of the addon's and
  every `ns.Later`; what runs each frame stops at its first error (`ns.EveryFrame`). The guards missing are R1 to R5.
- **A fight.** The bar's layout, a folder's layout, a drag, an undo, a macro saved or deleted, the bags' fold and
  what is made once all wait for a fight's end; the menus grey what waits. I found no call on a secure button nor on
  the bar that holds them outside those waits. Whether the game agrees is V2.
- **Saved data.** A newer version is refused whole; every field is checked at load, rings of folders broken, and what
  the game can't write back is dropped at logout. The gap is R6.
- **Work done only when asked.** No polling beyond the six tickers; redraws are asked for many times and done once a
  frame; `OnUpdate` runs only during a drag, a fold, or in Edit Mode (and the calendar's one cheap test).
- **A patch.** The login check tries what the addon takes from the game on the player's own client, per build, and
  the report says what changed since the last build.

## 3. The order I would take

1. V1: commit `hud/` and its breaks. Nothing else protects the rest.
2. V2: the fifteen minutes in game, before more is built on inference.
3. R3, R1, R2: each small, each a total or lasting loss when it happens.
4. R4: the wrapper and its offline check, so the report can be trusted to hold what failed.
5. R5, R6, R7, V3 and the smaller notes: an afternoon together.
6. M1 and M2 when the info bar is next extended (the layout per character with import and export would be the
   moment: it rewrites who owns the saved places anyway).
