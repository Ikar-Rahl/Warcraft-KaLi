# KaLi HUD: review of 2026-11-17

Asked by Ikar on 2026-11-17: "a full, thorough review of the KaLi hud plugin. Focus on robustness, and the info bar
usability. Propose enhancement gameplay wise, do not hesitate making user research on what they would like or not, the
goal here is to make an addon way and beyond what any other done in term of UX, usage simplicity, convenience."

Nothing in the addon was changed for this review. Every proposal is for Ikar to rule on.

**Status, later the same day.** Ikar: "Fix all the bugs." D1 to D5 and D7 to D12 are fixed, each with its test and
its deliberate breaks; `design.md` (The review of 2026-11-17) says how. D3's fix is a folder for what doesn't fit,
« Suite » (his "Maybe a special folder ?"); D4's is proposal P1 without its limit on long texts; D2's padlock opens
again after the fight (his "Yes, he reopen"). No proposal is built. Ruled the same day: D6 is no defect, the plate
redraws in Edit Mode only ("No, only in edit mode, I don't think the user will be happy with the new form without
changing the other bara"); P3, the mark on a folder, isn't wanted ("No need for now, the goal is not to have a
notification system"); nor P14, a key for a macro ("No, this is not the job of the info bar"). The text below is the
review as written, before the fixes and the rulings.

## In short

- **The code is sound and carefully defended**, and the family check passes. The review still found **twelve defects**,
  each shown by a probe on the fake game (two of them by forcing an error). The first comes back at every login in a
  common situation (a folder left open).
- **The largest robustness risk is not in the code**: nothing has run in the game yet. Taint and the secure frames'
  behaviour in a fight can only be seen there (section 2 gives a short test).
- **The info bar already does what no single addon does**: an info bar, a home for other addons' minimap buttons,
  macros, folders that open in a fight, undo. Titan Panel and MinimapButtonButton are fourth and tenth of the "ten
  most important addons" for WoW Forever in the one list read (mein-mmo, 2026-10-26): KaLi HUD does both jobs.
- **Where it falls short of "beyond any other"**: it lets things shift and overlap, a folder hides what needs
  attention inside it, some actions give no sign that they worked, and a layout can't differ by character nor be
  shared.
- **What players complain about most in such bars** is breakage after patches and things that move by themselves. The
  static plate, the API check at login and the tests answer exactly that. Finishing that job comes before new features.

  2026-11-16.
- **Not reviewed:** `Core/Geometry.lua`, `Core/Format.lua` and `UI/Border.lua` (the drawing and the numbers),

  hub's French texts.
- **Reproduced** means a probe started the addon on the repo's fake game, as `hud/tools/run_tests.py` does, and
  triggered it. The probes read the repo and write nothing in it. Their output is in the appendix.
- **Measured** means with the game's own font (`FRIZQT__.TTF`), the way `tools/kali/labels.py` measures labels.
- **Research:** about twenty public pages read on 2026-11-17 (listed at the end): forum threads, the user comments of
  one info-bar addon (80 comments, four of about 37 pages), the pages and comments of minimap-button collectors, three

## 1. Defects

Severity: **high**, a player meets it in ordinary play; **medium**, it needs a particular but plausible situation, or
breaks one of Ikar's own rules; **low**, rare or cheap.

**D1. An error at every login while a folder is saved open.**
- What the player meets: the red line in chat at each login or `/reload`. For that session the hub's start stops
  halfway: the macros' two handlers are never registered (a game macro renamed or deleted isn't followed, a macro
  whose button waited for a fight's end is never set), and nothing KaLi HUD makes once is made (the folders and macros
  it brings, and whatever a later version adds to them).
- How: leave a folder that opens on a click (the default) open, then reload or log out. The folder is saved open, so
  it comes back at every login until it is shut before leaving.
- Cause: `Folders.Setup` (`Folders.lua:379`) calls `onlyOpen`, a local function declared at line 529, after its use.
  At line 379 the name is a global, and nil. `Hub.Start` then runs `Folders.Setup`, `Macros.Start` and `Seeds.Run` one
  after the other with no guard between them, so the first failing stops the two others.
- Why the checks missed it: the restart check starts from the smoke's saved file, where no folder is open, and no
  deliberate break touches the end of `Setup`.
- Fix: declare `onlyOpen` before `Setup`; run `Hub.Start`'s three steps each under `ns.SafeCall`, as `Main.lua` runs
  the pieces; a smoke session that ends with a folder open, then its restart; a break for each.

**D2. A fight that starts with the padlock open.**
- What the player meets: the padlock can't be turned in a fight (by design), so the HUD stays in arranging mode until
  it ends. A macro's own right-click runs nothing for the whole fight (`*type2` is nil and can't be set then). Another
  addon's right-click never reaches the addon: it opens the hub menu, greyed.
- `design.md` says "In a fight the padlock can't open, so everything does what its author wrote". That holds only when
  it was shut as the fight began.
- Fix: as the fight starts (`PLAYER_REGEN_DISABLED`, where `InfoBar` already draws what waited and the game still
  accepts `SetAttribute`), shut the padlock and arm the macros. **To rule:** open it again after the fight if it was
  open, or leave it shut. Related: an open padlock is saved, so it stays open across sessions, which Edit Mode never
  does (proposal P9).

**D3. More on the bar than it is wide.**
- Cause: `placeBar` lays the left items from the left end and the right ones from the right end, and nothing checks
  that they meet. Only what came down from the pockets is dropped when the middle is short.
- Measured, French, text size 10: a first run needs about **950 units**. A 16:9 screen at the game's default UI scale
  is **1365 units** wide; Ikar's is 2100 (his probe's report), so his bar has half as much again.

  | Screen at the default UI scale | Size 10 | Size 14 | Size 20 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | 16:9, 1365 units | fits, 416 to spare | fits, 36 to spare | 534 short |
  | 4:3, 1024 units | fits, 75 to spare | 305 short | 875 short |
  | 21:9, 1792 units | fits | fits | 107 short |

- Other addons' data sources land on the bar's right side, each roughly 60 to 100 units (an estimate): on the 16:9
  screen five or six use up what is spare at size 10.
- Reproduced with data sources added on the fake: with 10 more, 5 items lie over the next and 4 are partly off the
  screen. The fake counts colour codes as letters, so its numbers overstate; the absence of any limit is the finding.
- Fix: proposal P2.

**D4. A text that gains or loses a character moves everything after it.**
- The rule it breaks is Ikar's: "nothing moves on a game event outside Edit Mode" (the mail's place, 2026-11-16).
- The first suspicion, a shift every second, is **dismissed by measurement**: the game's font has digits of one width
  (all 6.40 at size 10), so the clock and the coordinates never change width, and the performance text changes only
  when a number gains a digit.
- What remains: the performance text is 66.0 units wide at 99 images a second and 72.4 at 100. So a frame rate that
  hovers round 100, a latency round 100 ms, bags going from 9 to 10, gold, and any other addon's data source whose
  text varies in length. Performance stands at the right end by default: in the probe, six changes moved all five
  items of the right side six times.
- In a fight nothing may be moved, so a text that grows lies over its neighbour until the fight ends (`InfoBar.lua`'s
  header says so).
- Fix: proposal P1.

**D5. One element that fails to draw takes the whole bar with it.**
- Cause: `collect` and `placeBar` fill and place every element in one pass with no guard round each. An error in one
  aborts the layout. A folder's list is guarded (`ns.SafeCall(Folders.Layout)`); the bar isn't.
- Reproduced with an object made to fail: there at login, the bar came up with 12 buttons of 111; arriving later, the
  bar stopped following every change (gold moved to the right end stayed drawn on the left).
- No real object was found that triggers it today. This is about how much is lost the day one does: a patch, another
  addon's odd object, a bug of ours in one item.
- Fix: guard each element's fill; one that fails shows the question mark, is kept once in the error log, and the rest
  is laid out.

**D6. The plate and a layout changed outside Edit Mode. To rule.**
- The plate is static outside Edit Mode by Ikar's ruling (2026-11-15: "people do not expect their UI to change with
  dynamic events"). Two changes are not dynamic events but layout choices:
  - Blizzard's Edit Mode manager applies another layout when the specialisation changes
    (`Blizzard_EditMode/Shared/EditModeManager.lua`: `PLAYER_SPECIALIZATION_CHANGED`, then `UpdateLayoutInfo` and a
    line in chat). Whether Forever keeps a layout per specialisation is the game's to say.
  - The options' action bars page shows or hides bars 2 to 8 (`PROXY_SHOW_ACTIONBAR_n`).
- Reproduced: a bar the game moved 300 units stays drawn at its old place until `/kh redraw`.
- Proposal: freeze again once, on the next frame and out of a fight, when the active layout changes
  (`EDIT_MODE_LAYOUTS_UPDATED`) and when the options toggle a bar.

**D7. An error during a drag.**
- `Drag.lua:313` sets its `OnUpdate` itself, not through `ns.EveryFrame`, the rule of the 2026-11-16 review ("What
  runs every frame stops at its first error").
- Reproduced by forcing one: 60 errors in 60 frames, raised to the game unguarded, none in the addon's own log. On
  release `Drag.Finish` raises too and the hub stays "dragging" (no tooltip, the element under the pointer) until a
  fight starts or a reload.
- Fix: `ns.EveryFrame(watcher, tick, Drag.Cancel)`; `Finish` cancels when it can't read its target.

**D8. A minimap button made after login.** `Collect.Scan` runs at login, 2 s and 8 s later, and on LibDBIcon's own
callback. A button another addon makes later by itself (an addon loaded on demand) stays on the map until the next
login. "Buttons not collected" is the most frequent complaint in the comments of MBB (Minimap Button Bag), an older
collector (2008 to 2018). Fix: one scan a moment after each `ADDON_LOADED`, through `ns.Later`.

**D9. A right-click that does nothing.** Shut, a right-click on another addon's object is "its addon's" even when the
object has no click at all (a plain readout): nothing happens, and its placement is out of reach until the padlock is
opened. Fix: `Menus.Theirs` counts a LibDataBroker object only when it has an `OnClick`.

**D10. An open folder laid out again and again.** Five attributes changing with the attributes' folder open (37
members): its list laid out 5 times, 160 buttons filled; a full layout of the whole hub fills 98. Each folder opening
or shutting also asks for a full layout. Fix: mark the folder and lay it out once a frame.

**D11. What is published to LibDataBroker.** Every item of ours is published at login whether this character has it
or not: 95 objects in the test session, 31 that are another class's reagents or statistics the build doesn't know,
with no readable label. Another display beside KaLi HUD offers them all; Titan Panel is in the Forever lists. Fix:
publish what the character has, each with its label set first.

**D12. A key that isn't a name.** `DB.Open` checks each item's fields, not its key. A number as key, on a file from
before the folders, reaches `Folders.lua:339` (`name:find`) and stops the first setup. It needs a file edited by hand
or damaged. Fix: `DB.Open` drops a key that isn't a text, counted as repaired.

**Smaller notes, no probe:** the session's list of blocked actions (`Session.lua`) grows without limit; `Hub.Set`
is given a new colour table at each update (the session's, the errors'), so their buttons are drawn again each time;
`Collect`'s `twin` puts away for good a button whose name resembles a LibDataBroker object's, with no way back in the
menus if the two did different things.

**Checked and sound:** KaLi HUD as the only addon installed (no LibStub, no LibDataBroker, no other button): a clean
login and a clean short session. The saved data's repair is thorough. The digits' width (above).

## 2. What only the game can say

Nothing here can be judged offline. In this order, about fifteen minutes:

1. **Taint.** `/console taintLog 1`, then: open Edit Mode from the bar's menu (the addon calls `ShowUIPanel`), pick the
   KaLi HUD strip (it calls `ClearSelectedSystem`), leave Edit Mode, fight and use the action bars; unlock, open the
   tracking menu through its handle (`SetMenuOpen` from addon code) and pick a tracking; fold and unfold the bags
   (`BagsBar:Layout` and `ToggleExpandBar` from addon code), then use an item from a bag in a fight. Read
   `Logs/taint.log` for KaLiHUD, and watch for the game's message that an addon's action was blocked.
2. **A fight:** a folder holding macros opens and shuts, its macros run, a macro's right-click runs
   (`design.md`'s open questions list the rest).
3. **A reload in a fight:** whether the bar is there at once. If `InCombatLockdown()` is already true at
   `PLAYER_LOGIN`, the bar waits, empty, for the fight's end.
4. `/kh test`, then `/khprobe` and `/reload`.

## 3. What players say (research digest)

- **What Forever's players are told to install.** mein-mmo's ten (2026-10-26): Questie, DungeonJournal, Leatrix Plus,
  **Titan Panel**, Details!, Baganator, What's Training?, AtlasLoot, Forever Quest Tint, **MinimapButtonButton**. Of
  Titan Panel: "everything is customizable. You decide what you want to see and what you don't." Of the minimap:
  "you can hardly find or click on individual buttons"; MinimapButtonButton costs one more click to reach a button.
- **The taste of the moment is one suite and a short list.** Luxthos (2026-11-10): "I wanted to keep my AddOn list
  short", "a very clean Forever interface", "I don't want the same information twice". Koroboost: "Set it up once and
  forget it", to avoid "configuring twenty separate addons"; a patch can break "half your addons". Both recommend
  EllesmereUI, one suite for the whole interface.
- **The info-bar field is thin on Forever.** Arcana (ChocolateBar renamed): two users asking for a Forever version
  (2026-09-20, 2026-10-12). Titan Panel: one site lists it for Forever "via TOC workaround", from a single user's tip;
  its own page lists as a known issue that plugins can disappear after a reload if dragged.
- **What users of such a bar complain about** (Arcana, 80 comments read): errors after a patch (9), Blizzard's bars
  shifting because the addon moves them (6: "the main action bar moves, but is constantly shifting"; "It was driving
  me crazy too"), no updates (4), bars gone after a patch (3), bars that can't be moved or deleted (3), CPU use (1),
  settings lost (1), a blocked action (1).
- **What they ask for** in that sample: a fade or opacity on mouseover (3), a font outline or size (2), moving plugins
  (1, answered "Drag and drop").
- **What the established bars list as features:** a fixed width for any plugin (ChocolateBar), a limit on a plugin's
  text width and its colour (Bazooka), a label and an icon switch per block and a maximum width (Button Bin), hiding
  in a fight or until the pointer is on it (all three), profiles copied between characters (Titan Panel), profiles
  shared as text (JulsanityUI DataBars).
- **The modules every bar has** (Titan Panel, XIV_Databar, JulsanityUI): clock, gold, performance, durability,
  experience; most: bags, volume, mail, location. KaLi HUD has all of them.
- **Minimap collectors.** MinimapButtonButton: 18.7 million downloads on its page; buttons included or ignored by a
  slash command, found with `/fstack`. The comments of an older collector, MBB (Minimap Button Bag, 2008 to 2018):
  buttons not collected or wrongly caught (6), buttons that vanish (4), a stutter (2008, 2018), one taint error. Newer
  collectors advertise a live search, favourites, categories, an inbox for new buttons, closing after a click, a count
  per folder.
- **Macros.** Players ask Blizzard for more than the game's 120 and 18 slots (two forum threads), and addons sell
  storage beyond them and keys without slots. These were seen as search summaries only, not read in full.

Three things follow for KaLi HUD:

1. **Stability is the feature.** What people complain about most is what the plate's design, the API check and the
   tests are for. D1 to D7 are that job unfinished.
2. **Right with nothing touched.** "One addon", "set it up once": the first run has to be right, and nothing a player
   needs should hide.
3. **The customising people use is small:** what shows, where, how wide, icon or text, and a layout per character.

## 4. Proposals

Each: what was seen, the proposal, why, its size, and whether it needs a ruling. **Now** is small and safe; **next**
is worth a design pass; **later** is larger.

### The bar holds still

**P1. Places that hold (now; fixes D4).**
- Each item keeps a place as wide as the widest it has shown since the player last arranged the bar (login, a drag,
  its menu, the padlock, Edit Mode). A text that grows widens its place once; one that shrinks leaves it.
- Another addon's text is cut past some length (say 24 letters, then « … »), whole in its tooltip, so one talkative
  data source can't push the bar about.
- No new option. In a fight a growing text already has its room.
- Size: small (`flush` ignores a narrower width; the places kept by name, forgotten on those occasions).

**P2. Never over another (next; fixes D3).**
- When a side doesn't fit: what came down from the pockets goes first (as today), then the side's last items are held
  back, and a small « +3 » stands in their place. Its tooltip names them; a click opens them as a list.
- Step 1, small: held back, counted, named. Step 2, medium: the list (a folder the HUD makes on the spot, never saved).
- The size slider in Edit Mode then shows it happen instead of drawing texts over one another.

### The bar tells

**P3. Folders that tell (next; the proposal with most to give in play).**
- Seen: 91 of a first run's 118 elements live in folders (the test session), and a folder's button says nothing of
  them. Reagents at 0,
  no ammunition, an item at 10 %, an invitation, an error: all behind an icon until it is opened.
- Proposal: an item of ours may say it needs attention, by states the game's own UI already marks: a count at 0
  (reagents, ammunition), durability under 20 %, bags full, an invitation pending, an error this session. Its folder's
  button then wears a small mark, a shape as well as a colour, that appears once without motion and goes with its
  cause. An icon-only folder's tooltip adds « 12 éléments » and what needs attention.
- Why: the player learns he is out of reagents before the pull, not during it; and "glance, hover, click" holds for
  what a folder holds.
- Size: medium. **To rule:** which states count.

**P4. What you asked for is shown to you (now).**
- Seen: ticking an element in the bar's menu puts it back where it was, often inside a shut folder. Nothing visible
  happens. An undo is the same.
- Proposal: the element flashes once where it lands (half a second, as the game marks a new item); in a shut folder,
  the folder's button flashes and its list opens, out of a fight.
- Size: small.

**P5. New arrivals (next).**
- Seen: a new addon's launcher lands in « Add-ons » unannounced; its data source lands on the bar and shifts it. A
  player with no other addon has an empty « Add-ons » folder from the first day (probe: 0 members).
- Proposal: the folder wears a "new" mark until it is opened, its new members until hovered. The folder is made when
  the first thing lands in it. **To rule:** new data sources into the folder too (the ruling of 2026-11-14 put them on
  the bar), so that nothing appears on the bar unasked.

### One gesture less

**P6. Pick, and it shuts (next).** A launcher or a macro clicked in a list opened by a click leaves the list open; a
menu shuts when you choose. Proposal: such a list shuts after a click on a launcher or a macro, out of a fight (in one
it already shuts once left). Size: small.

**P7. Settings where you are (now).** « Mode Édition » in the bar's menu opens the dialog on « Style ». Proposal: on
the info bar's section, as a status bar's box already opens its own. Size: tiny.

**P8. Take a button the HUD missed (next; goes with D8).** « Prendre un bouton… » in the bar's menu, then a click on
any button on the screen takes it, with the checks `Collect` already makes (not the game's, not protected).
MinimapButtonButton asks for `/fstack` and a typed name for this. Size: small to medium.

**P9. The padlock shuts itself (now; goes with D2).** In a fight; and at login, as Edit Mode never stays open across
a reload. **To rule.**

### The player's own choices

**P10. The game's own clock (now).**
- Seen: « Format 24 heures » and « Afficher l'heure du royaume » are options of ours beside the game's own two
  (`timeMgrUseMilitaryTime`, `timeMgrUseLocalTime`, set in the clock's window, which a click on our clock opens), and
  can disagree with them. The date is written day, month, year in every language.
- Proposal: read the game's (`GameTime_GetTime`, `FormatShortDate`, both in this build) and drop our two options. A
  player on an English client gets 12 hours and month first with nothing touched.
- Size: small.

**P11. « Affichage » for every element (next).** Icon and text, icon alone or text alone exist for folders and macros.
Proposal: for ours and other addons' objects too, saved with the element's place. Three established bars have a
switch of this kind per plugin. Size: small to medium.

**P12. Gold: forget a character (now).** Every character ever logged in stays in gold's list and its total; one deleted
or renamed can't be removed. Proposal: « Personnages » in gold's menu, a tick each. Size: small.

**P13. Layouts as Edit Mode's (later).** One layout serves the whole account, and a game macro named in it may not
exist on another character. Proposal: « Propre à ce personnage » (it starts as a copy), « Copier depuis… », and
« Partager… », a text to paste, read back through `DB.Open`'s repair so a bad text can do no harm. Size: large.

### In play

**P14. A key for a macro, a key for a folder (next). To rule.**
- Seen: the HUD's macros are kept by the HUD, not in the game's 120 and 18 slots. The player already has more macros
  than the game allows, and nothing says so. They can only be clicked.
- Proposal: « Raccourci… » in a macro's menu takes a key and binds it to its button's click (the buttons then need
  names; set out of a fight, saved for the account). A folder's key opens it.
- Ikar's test of 2026-11-16 was "no gaming shortcuts that make the player a bot". A key that clicks one macro is the
  game's own mechanism, one press for one click; the ruling is his.
- Size: medium.

**P15. First run (next).**
- Seen: thirteen things on the bar's left, nine of them icons told apart only by hovering. Experience shows twice, as
  an item and as progress bar 1.
- Proposal: the experience item hidden by default while a progress bar shows experience. One help tip of Blizzard's
  own (`HelpTip`) on the padlock, the first time: « Clic droit sur la barre : choisir ce qu'elle montre. Le cadenas :
  tout déplacer. », gone at the first click, never again. No line in chat.
- Size: small.

### Considered and not proposed

- **Hiding or fading the bar** (every other bar has it; three comments ask for more of it): the info bar is the plate's
  foot, the HUD's base, not an overlay.
- **A second bar at the top** (Titan Panel's usual place): the plate is at the bottom by the first ruling.
- **Fonts and textures of the bar's own:** the game's font follows the client's language and the player's settings.
- **A search in folders:** worth it past thirty members or so; later, if « Add-ons » grows that large in use.
- **Performance's click** (`collectgarbage`): forcing a collection frees nothing the game wouldn't and costs a hitch.
  Worth replacing by nothing, or by the game's addon list.

### The order I would take

1. D1, D2 with P9, D5, D7, D9, D12: an afternoon, each with its smoke case and its break.
2. P1, D10, D8, D11, P7, P4, P10, P12.
3. The test in the game (section 2), before anything larger is built on what hasn't been seen.
4. P2, P3, P6, P11, P15, P5, P8.
5. P14, D6, P13.

**Rulings needed:** D2 and P9 (the padlock after a fight, and at login); D6 (the plate and layouts changed outside
Edit Mode); P3 (which states need attention); P5 (new data sources into the folder); P14 (keys for macros).

## 5. Proposals, second round: in play (2026-11-17, evening)

Ikar, once the defects were fixed: "I didn't hear from you any enhancement proposal, other than a glow when activating
an element. Not talking about the previous 3 that were bad ideas. This is a bit thin." The reply to the review had put
five proposals in front of him and left ten in this file; none was about play. These thirteen are, or are the ones of
section 4 still worth a ruling. Each kept to his own tests (2026-11-16: nothing that belongs in a spell slot, no
serious tracking or data storage, no shortcut that makes the player a bot; 2026-11-17: no notification system, no keys,
the plate static). Each reads calls this build's source has (named below); none was tried in the game.

1. **Shift-click says it in the chat.** With the chat line open, a Shift-click on any readout writes it there, as the
   game does for an item (`ChatFrameUtil.InsertLink`). The coordinates give the game's own clickable map pin
   (`C_Map.GetUserWaypointHyperlink`); a reagent or the ammunition its item link and count; the others their name and
